Starter nest, seen from above

A small 3D printed tray for moving a young colony out of its test tube. The outworld and the nest sit side by side, each under its own drop-in lid. The test tube plugs into a socket, and a water tower keeps the last chamber damp through a printed grille, so no wick is needed.

Checks

Summary

Print and cut files

The tray and plugs come out as STL files. Acrylic lids come out as SVG cut files, using the kerf and colours from the Nest panel page. A glass lid only needs the size in the summary.

The gauge is a plate of holes from 13 to 20mm in 0.5mm steps, five to a row, starting at the cut corner. The smallest hole your tube slides into is its outside diameter.
